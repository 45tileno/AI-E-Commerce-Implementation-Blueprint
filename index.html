Phase 4: Tax Deduction E-Receipt Logic

Detailing the custom logic required to generate the "Rise Our Youth" compliant tax documents. This runs automatically in the background after a successful payment.

Generation Process

  • 1

    Data Capture

    Extract customer_name, amount, and address from the Stripe Session Metadata.

  • 2

    PDF Compilation

    Use react-pdf to inject data into the "Rise Our Youth" template. Assets (Logo/Signatures) are base64 encoded.

  • 3

    Delivery

    SendGrid API triggers email with PDF attachment immediately.
